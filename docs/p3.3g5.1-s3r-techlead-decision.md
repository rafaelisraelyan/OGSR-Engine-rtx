## Blocking evidence findings (Codex)

**P1-A — invalid crash provenance / ALife save-load:** `logs/savecheck-on/savecheck-on.log` is actually from **`xrEngine_m1_on.exe -dx12_game_bridge -log`**, not the alleged S3R save-load process; no `CALifeGraphRegistry::add` stack/dump in supplied archive and no actual feature-off paired crash. JSON `0xC0000005` alone does not establish root cause. `g51_s3r_existing` had **1 PhysX + 10 ODE** in the ordinary NPC population; after load the previously PhysX-owned NPC **13357 is absent**, so its ODE-corpse restoration is **not demonstrated**. Prior S2R2 real saves successfully load ODE corpses, but cannot substitute for this missing handoff case. No confirmed production PhysX bug assigned.

**P1-B — dropped steps and incorrect frame-time semantics:** `perf-on-3` has **178 dropped physics steps**, **49** smoothed `Device.fTimeDelta` values >50ms, yet **60** intervals >50ms from consecutive `time_ms` timestamps. Device.fTimeDelta has smoothing/clamping and must not be published as *raw frame wall time*. In `campaign-on-3`, cumulative dropped count reaches **542** (not one-window fresh count). PhysX SceneStep call duration remains relatively small; direct PhysX cost, indirect worker contention, GPU, harness and external causes are **not isolated**. Include all 4 PhysX runs in interpretation; no unproved “external hitch” dismissal. CPU means from `QueryThreadCycleTime` refer to the invoking thread, not aggregate workers; ODE `FrameStep` is wall timing. WPO/LTCG is off; optimized production budget not established.

**P1-C — static coverage and real-population fallback:** ordinary-population `campaign-existing-on` ended **1 PhysX + 10 ODE**, `S3R_CHECKPOINT_FAIL` four times and `failed=1` despite OS exit 0. For ten, logs report static tile **73116 source triangles > 16384 configured limit** and safe ODE fallback. This is not a failure of fallback correctness, but a major limit on PhysX applicability to real game terrain. Investigate geometry cooking/selection/partition and coverage before a rollout decision; do **not** simply increase the cap without budget/contacts/accuracy evidence.

**P2 — remaining functional coverage:** rig metadata comparison/pose handoff continuity, per-rig contacts and joint limits, actual in-game loot UI, representative ALife spawn/save/teardown, negative static-coverage edge cases. Slot reuse NOT RUN. Historical UI 70/70 and older DX12 CPU prebuilt checks are not S3R fresh regressions; DX12 GPU not run.
