# P2.2A - PhysX Ragdoll Mass, Stability & Settling

> **Superseded by `docs/p2.2a-fix-physx-ragdoll.md`.** The energy/contact
> numbers and the impulse description below predate the P2.2A-FIX corrections.
> Keep this file as the historical P2.2A record; use the FIX report for the
> current implementation and gate status.

**Status: PASS on the stability objective; PARTIAL on the media criterion.**
The synthetic root mass and impulse mapping were the cause of the excessive
motion. The root now gets mass/inertia from its real collision shapes (9.876 kg,
total 92.213 kg), the impulse is applied to the pelvis root (body 0), damping and
solver settings were raised, and the real NPC ragdoll settles with zero kinetic
energy, sleeps, and keeps joint anchors within 0.4 mm. All physics, lifecycle
and regression checks pass. A new isolated before/after video was not obtained
because the archive camera and the actor overlap the single live NPC; numerical
graphs, CSV and screenshots are provided.

## Root cause

The real capture has body 0 as `root_stalker`:

- role `StaticRoot`, ODE mass `0`, two collision spheres, merged bones
  `bip01`, `bip01_pelvis`, `bip01_spine`, `bip01_tail`;
- P2.1 promoted it in game code to `0.5 kg` with inertia `0.01`, which is not a
  physical mass for the pelvis/spine;
- `bodyDynamic[0]` was also false because the root is `isFixed`, so the hit
  impulse was silently redirected to body 1 (left thigh, 7.46 kg);
- the scene used solver 8/2, no linear/angular damping, and did not suppress
  non-adjacent self-collisions;
- the old P2.1 A/B line reached **51.2 rad/s**, **0.305 m** joint anchor error,
  **0.759 rad** limit error and 11/11 awake bodies after 6 s.

## Fix

- The PhysX backend now promotes the static root itself:
  `updateMassAndInertia(root, density=300 kg/m^3)` from the real two-sphere
  geometry. Result: **root 9.876 kg**, **total 92.213 kg**, root body dynamic.
- The impulse now goes to body 0 (pelvis root), not to a thigh.
- Runtime damping: linear `0.05`, angular `0.2`.
- Solver: **16 position / 4 velocity** iterations (A/B best balance; 24/8 lowered
  anchor error further but had a higher transient angular peak).
- Same-ragdoll self-collision is suppressed; adjacent jointed bodies were
  already non-colliding in PhysX.
- Energy/contact diagnostics were added to `PhysXRagdollWorld`; the game logs
  KE, PE, total energy, max angular speed, anchor error, limit error, contacts,
  penetration, awake bodies and impulse count.
- `CCharacterPhysicsSupport::UpdatePhysXRagdoll` now calls `spatial_move()` so
  the renderer follows a ragdoll that moves away from its spawn sector.
- The smoke harness suppresses post-activation external hits, so the energy
  trace is not contaminated by other NPCs shooting the corpse.

steps (6 s), 40 N*s reproducible impulse for the impulse variants.

The root mass fix alone reduces the angular peak from 51 to 23 rad/s and the
joint anchor error from 30.5 cm to 1.7 cm. Solver 16/4 gives the best angular
peak (17.3 rad/s) and a 57 mrad limit error. Density 300 is the chosen
compromise: 92.2 kg total is plausible for a geared stalker; density 500 adds
6.6 kg to the root and raises the energy peak.

## Known limitations

- The synthetic pelvis/spine root is assigned mass from its collision volume at
  300 kg/m3; this is a documented shape-density approximation, not an ODE mass
  readback (the ODE root mass is zero by contract).
- One active ragdoll per level; only one live NPC exists in the test map, so
  in-game slot reuse is covered by the C ABI recreate test, not by a second NPC.
- Self-collision is suppressed for the whole ragdoll; limbs can interpenetrate
  visually. Adjacent jointed pairs were already non-colliding in PhysX.
- The in-game energy log has `contacts=0` while the body sleeps; contact events
  are not reported for the resting/sleeping island in this harness. The A/B
  tool reports contact counts/impulses during the fall.
- The archive camera cannot reliably isolate a clean before/after video; this is
  why the media criterion remains PARTIAL.
- No save/load, no animation-driven ragdoll, no other NPCs, no IX-Ray, no DX12.
