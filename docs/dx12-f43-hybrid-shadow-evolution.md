## P0-B: adaptive local shadow resources (planned; no GPU implementation)

Retain existing banked fallback until parity. Current GameDx12ShadowViews/LocalShadowAllocator and SceneFrameConstants hard-code 16 candidates, 3 banks, 2 spots + 1 point per bank, 1024x1024 slices, 28 projection IDs and 32-bit view mask. Replacing only allocator is insufficient. Audit all capture/admission/CBV/HLSL/list/timing limits. Design dynamic light and shadow-view buffers, variable-size groups and tiered pages/array or atlas (choose after measured fragmentation, PCF guard-band and draw scheduling trade-offs), budget from DXGI QueryVideoMemoryInfo plus allocation sizes; do not treat reported budget as a reservation. Dynamic admitted lights and complete six-face point allocations, stable ID/generation, deterministic priority, quantized resolution with hysteresis; downgrade resolution before shedding; pages grow subject to real memory/frame-time budget with explicit pressure and telemetry. Preserve ownership across epoch/reset and fence completion; no overwrite in-flight tiles/descriptors. For atlas, guard bands must cover actual filter footprint and raster projection must include them, with per-tile viewport/scissor and bounded sampling. Depth clear must be tile-local, no whole-page clear destroying neighbors. Publish only complete valid projections and handle point seams. Under pressure use explicit fallback policy (retain visibility, lower res, suppress or alternative shadowing) rather than silently allowing shadowed lights to leak through walls. No fake unlimited memory promise.

Separate light admission from shadow budgeting; update HLSL metadata, root signature/descriptor and FrameCB ABI together, move variable arrays to structured buffers with validated ranges. Replace bitmask/list fixed capacity with scalable view work plan; schedule GR chunks and command-list pools per slot without one allocator per unlimited light; preserve deterministic submission/barriers and fence reuse. Validate source identity, resize, level reload, OOM/device loss, negative stale handles, mixed spot/point, many shadow lights, nearest light stability, seam/PCF borders, frame and memory pressure. No arbitrary candidate cutoff. CPU allocation tests first as evidence, not production restrictions; then native GPU binding and real in-game parity before switching defaults.

Investigate depth caching only after correctness, and GPU-driven/LOD for costly far cascades. HUD screen-space contact shadows already required in F4.5-B; future DXR contact/soft shadows share visibility contracts without double shadowing. Current CSM fitting, overlap, texel snapping, receiver-plane mode, GR job system and R4 path retained. No new GPU timings or visual claims.
