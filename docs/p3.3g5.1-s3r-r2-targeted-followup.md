### R2-C (P1) — static geometry resource safety before any production proposal

1. Source audit `readStaticFragmentTile`, `ensureStaticTileAt`, `sceneAddStaticFragment`, scene lifetime/leases, cache generation and release. Map case coverage over **at least three meaningful actual game areas if available**, including dense, empty and cross-tile boundary locations. Use ordinary existing ALife NPCs and controlled real-world characters, not artificial bodies. Include both baseline caps and experimental raised limits, same level/save when applicable.
2. Preserve **fail-closed ODE fallback** and validate truly correct contacts/coverage: no holes caused by centroid assignment, boundary triangles, out-of-tile geometry, empty tile semantics, partial successful inserts followed by later failure, repeated rejected dense tile, repeated local deaths, static mesh actor lifetime while ragdolls remain active. No automatic "empty tile = safe pass" policy.
3. Quantify source/cooked/owned/cached triangle counters, extraction/cooking wall p50/p95/p99/max, dynamic allocation/high-water memory, per-scene and per-level cached actor and shape counts, repeated rejects, wall spikes (60ms tile), NPC capture coverage per area; include same-process level unload/reload/revisit and at least one longer session. 4. Compare: (i) the **three-constant budget-only proposal** under bounded realistic loads vs (ii) a minimally scoped **bounded cache/streaming/negative cache with generation-safe invalidation** design. Prepare a design note and isolated optional proposal; no production merge. Watch that accepted tiles persist after a subsequent rejected tile and eventually consume budget, no eviction at baseline; check testable policy for safe fallback.
5. Do not claim 11/11 generalizes to 11 different skeleton families: R1 used **3 visual paths** and did not establish pose/contact continuity.
